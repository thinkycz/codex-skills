# Compare

Compare specification, plan, tasks and acceptance checks bidirectionally. Identify omitted requirements, unsupported additions, contradictions and unverifiable completion claims with source locations. Do not silently rewrite sources or assume newer implementation overrides agreed requirements. Propose the smallest correction and identify the decision owner.
