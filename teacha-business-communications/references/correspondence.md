# Partner correspondence

- Read the source message and relevant recent thread. Preserve branch-specific commission terms, amounts/currency, dates and the distinction between an offered condition and an accepted commitment. An offer for one branch does not apply to another automatically.
- Follow the user's actual negotiation intent. Thanking a partner for improved terms and considering another branch must not become acceptance of exclusivity or a promise to launch. Do not disclose a named competitor's terms when the requested comparison is general.
- Match the requested language and degree of warmth. Use natural opening/closing and enough context for the recipient; do not force every reply into a terse status message or invent contacts, capacity, locations or signatures.
- Draft in chat or the requested email editor. If saving in an app, verify recipient, subject, text and draft state. Send only within explicit current authorization; after uncertain sending, inspect Sent/thread state before retrying.
- A request covering several partners needs independent recipient/thread checks. Report each draft or sent result without implying a commercial agreement was completed.
