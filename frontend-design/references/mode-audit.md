# Audit

Inspect representative screens, interaction states and canonical in-product examples. Rank concrete defects by impact, frequency and repair scope. Distinguish product inconsistency from subjective style preferences. Audit mode is read-only: deliver evidence and priorities, not unsolicited redesign edits. Avoid replacing established shells, tabs or overlays with generic components merely for novelty.

## Selected detail

For nested panels, unreachable actions or clipped overlays, compare the named canonical screen at realistic window sizes and long-content states. Inspect useful workspace, persistent action reachability, clipping/stacking, focus entry/return, scrolling, Escape and outside-click dismissal. Read [the audit rubric](audit-audit-rubric.md) when these interactions are part of the report. Recommend only; audit mode does not authorize redesign edits.
